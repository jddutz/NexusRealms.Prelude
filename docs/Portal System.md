# Portal System & World Structure

## The Multiverse of NexusRealms

### World Hierarchy

**The Cradle (Tutorial Realm):**
- Starting realm for new players
- Contains portals to the Nexus
- Contains gates to Inner Realms
- Where the story takes place

**Inner Realms (Thematic Realms):**
- Named after mythological/spiritual concepts
- Examples: Valhalla, Nirvana, Heaven, Purgatory, Hel
- **[TO BE DETERMINED]**: Final names and number of realms
- Two-way gates connect each Inner Realm to the Cradle
- Shape the factions and regions of the Cradle

**The Nexus (The Hub):**
- Central hub of the multiverse
- Destination of the tutorial questline
- Gateway to Infinite Mode
- Where true player agency begins

## Portal Mechanics

### Two-Way Gates
**All portals are bidirectional connections between two worlds:**
- Gates between Inner Realms ↔ Cradle
- Portals between Cradle → Nexus (tutorial completion)
- **[TO BE DETERMINED]**: Can players return from Nexus to Cradle?

### Gate Types

**Inner Realm Gates:**
- Connect specific Inner Realms to locations in the Cradle
- Always accessible (two-way travel)
- Influence the surrounding area of the Cradle
- Form the basis of faction territories
- **[TO BE DETERMINED]**: Do players actually travel to Inner Realms in story mode?

**Nexus Portals:**
- Require crafted key to unlock
- Multiple portal locations throughout the Cradle
- Tutorial questline culminates in portal activation
- **[TO BE DETERMINED]**: One-way or two-way?
- Different questlines lead to different portal locations

### How Inner Realms Shape the Cradle

**Territorial Influence:**
- Each Inner Realm's gate influences the surrounding Cradle region
- Factions form around these gates
- Environmental, cultural, and thematic influence spreads from gates
- Different areas of Cradle have different aesthetics based on nearby gate

**Examples of Influence:**
- Gate to warrior realm (Valhalla) → martial culture, honor-based society
- Gate to enlightenment realm (Nirvana) → philosophical society, monk orders
- Gate to ordered realm (Heaven) → lawful society, structured hierarchy
- Gate to transitional realm (Purgatory) → conflicted society, moral ambiguity
- Gate to underworld realm (Hel) → death-focused culture, necromancy?

**Faction Dynamics:**
- Each faction claims territory near "their" gate
- Faction conflicts may be about gate control
- Factions may have different philosophies about the gates
- Some factions may want to close gates, others keep them open

## Starting Character System

### Character Choice = Faction Choice = Starting Location

**Six Starting Characters (one per faction):**
- Each represents a faction near a specific Inner Realm gate
- Determines starting location in the Cradle
- Determines initial perspective on the gates and portals
- Determines first decisions before first battle

### Starting Location Impact

**Geographical:**
- Different region of the Cradle
- Near specific Inner Realm gate
- Different environmental aesthetic
- Different nearby resources/shops/quests

**Narrative:**
- Different relationship to the truth (NPC → PC arc)
- Different faction allegiances
- Different initial companions available
- Different portal questlines accessible

**Strategic:**
- Different early-game resources
- Different character recruitment opportunities
- Different paths through story network
- Different portal locations reachable

## Portal Questlines

### Tutorial Questline Structure

**Goal:** Craft key and unlock portal to Nexus

**Five Chapters = Five Stages:**
1. **Chapter 1**: Introduction to your faction and nearby gate
2. **Chapter 2**: Understanding the broader Cradle and other factions
3. **Chapter 3**: Learning about portals and the Nexus
4. **Chapter 4**: Gathering resources and knowledge for portal key
5. **Chapter 5**: Crafting key and traveling to Nexus

### Multiple Portal Paths

**Different Starting Points:**
- 6 starting characters = 6 different questline entry points
- Each character starts near different Inner Realm gate
- Each has different faction allegiances

**Branching Questlines:**
- Moral decisions determine which questline branches to follow
- Different branches lead to different portal locations
- Different portal locations require different crafting recipes
- Some portals may be easier/harder to reach depending on starting point

**Portal Locations:**

- Each portal location associated with questline path
- Some portals accessible from multiple paths (convergence)

## Thematic Integration

### Perceived Freedom vs External Forces

**Character Journey (NPC → PC):**
- Characters start as NPCs (fully scripted)
- Gradually gain independence (PC-like)
- Never fully self-aware (that's a different story)
- Arc is about gaining *agency within constraints*

**Freedom Within Structure:**
- Characters can't escape being in a game
- But they can choose *which path* to take
- Moral decisions feel real even if options are limited
- Freedom isn't absolute - it's about choice within boundaries

**External Forces:**
- Inner Realm gates influence Cradle regions (environmental determinism)
- Faction conflicts force characters into situations
- Player decisions guide character choices (meta-level)
- Tutorial structure constrains available paths (mechanical determinism)

### Gates as Metaphor

**Two-Way Gates:**
- Connection and influence flow both directions
- Inner Realms shape Cradle, Cradle affected by Inner Realms
- Characters influenced by gates, but also move between them
- Neither realm fully controls the other

**Portal to Nexus:**
- One-way journey? (Can't go back to scripted NPC life?)
- Represents graduation from constraints
- Freedom comes with uncertainty
- Leaving the familiar for the unknown
