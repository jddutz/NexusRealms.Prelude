# Shadow Faction

**Alignment:** Lawful Evil

**Core Concept:** Masters of subterfuge, precision, and pragmatism who operate from darkness using stealth, deception, and calculated strikes.

**Hero Archetype:** The skilled rogue/assassin who achieves goals through cunning, preparation, and striking from the shadows with deadly efficiency.

**Connected Inner Realm:** [Underworld-inspired] - Realm of shadows, secrets, and hidden truths

---

## Visual Identity

### Aesthetic
- **Color Palette:** Black, dark gray, deep purple, blood red accents
- **Architecture:** Underground passages, hidden chambers, shadowy streets
- **Environmental Influence:** Area near gate has perpetual twilight, hidden passages, secrets everywhere
- **Armor/Clothing Style:** Light leather, cloaks, hoods, masks, dark clothing
- **Weapon Types:** Daggers, short swords, crossbows, poisons, throwing weapons

### Available Races
- Human (most common)
- Elf (Dark/Drow)
- Halfling
- Tiefling
- Half-Elf

---

## Starting Archetypes

### Archetype 1: Assassin
**Role:** Burst DPS (Stealth)  
**Starting Skill:** Backstab - Massive damage from stealth/behind enemy  
**Playstyle:** High burst damage, eliminating priority targets  
**Portrait Concept:** Hooded figure with daggers, emerging from shadows

### Archetype 2: Shadowstalker
**Role:** Scout/Mobile DPS  
**Starting Skill:** Shadow Step - Teleport short distance, enter stealth  
**Playstyle:** Mobile striker, hit-and-run tactics  
**Portrait Concept:** Cloaked figure mid-movement, fading into darkness

### Archetype 3: Saboteur
**Role:** Utility/Area Denial  
**Starting Skill:** Deploy Trap - Set trap that triggers on enemies  
**Playstyle:** Area control through traps, bombs, and debuffs  
**Portrait Concept:** Alchemist with vials and devices, crafting explosives

## Thematic Skill Pool

### Core Skills (Shadow Identity)

1. **Backstab**
   - Type: Offensive (Burst)
   - Description: Massive damage attack from behind or from stealth
   - Unlock: Starting skill for Assassin archetype

2. **Shadow Step**
   - Type: Utility/Mobility
   - Description: Teleport short distance, become hidden
   - Unlock: Starting skill for Shadowstalker archetype

3. **Deploy Trap**
   - Type: Utility/Area Denial
   - Description: Set trap (spike, net, explosive) that triggers on enemies
   - Unlock: Starting skill for Saboteur archetype

4. **Poison Blade**
   - Type: Offensive (DoT)
   - Description: Apply deadly poison to weapon, damage over time
   - Unlock: Chapter 2, Toxins Training mission

5. **Vanish**
   - Type: Utility/Defensive
   - Description: Disappear from combat temporarily, remove threat
   - Unlock: Chapter 2, Stealth Mastery quest

6. **Smoke Bomb**
   - Type: Utility/AoE
   - Description: Create smoke cloud, obscure vision, enable escapes
   - Unlock: Chapter 3, Alchemy Basics mission

7. **Sap**
   - Type: Utility/Control
   - Description: Knockout enemy from stealth, temporary disable
   - Unlock: Chapter 3, Silent Takedown training

8. **Shadow Clone**
   - Type: Summon/Utility
   - Description: Create illusory duplicate to confuse enemies
   - Unlock: Chapter 4, Shadow Arts quest

9. **Assassinate**
   - Type: Offensive (Execute)
   - Description: Instant kill or massive damage on low-health target
   - Unlock: Chapter 4, Perfect Kill questline

10. **Death Mark**
    - Type: Offensive/Debuff
    - Description: Mark target for death, all allies deal bonus damage
    - Unlock: Chapter 5, Master Assassin achievement

### Common Skills (Overlap)

1. **Pick Lock**
   - Type: Utility
   - Description: Unlock doors, chests, access hidden areas
   - Also Available From: Technology (hacking version)
   - Unlock: Chapter 1, Lockpicking Basics

2. **Detect Traps**
   - Type: Utility
   - Description: Reveal traps and hidden dangers
   - Also Available From: Arcane (detect magic), Technology (scanning)
   - Unlock: Chapter 2, Awareness Training

3. **Steal**
   - Type: Utility
   - Description: Pilfer items from enemies or environment
   - Also Available From: None (Shadow exclusive in most paths)
   - Unlock: Chapter 2, Thievery Guild initiation

### Rare Skills (Hard to Access)

1. **Perfect Strike**
   - Type: Offensive (Ultimate)
   - Description: Guaranteed critical hit with massive multiplier
   - Unlock: Chapter 5, Blade Mastery secret path

2. **Shadow Meld**
   - Type: Utility/Ultimate
   - Description: Become completely untargetable for brief time, perfect escape
   - Unlock: Chapter 5, Shadow Realm path

## Story Integration

### Starting Location
**Region:** The Twilight Reaches (Southeast Cradle)  
**Nearby Gate:** The Umbral Gate (Underworld realm connection)  
**Starting Settlement:** Duskmire - Hidden city in perpetual twilight, thieves' paradise

### Narrative Themes
- Pragmatism and efficiency over morality
- Secrets and hidden knowledge
- Survival through adaptation and cunning
- The value of information and intelligence
- Order through underground structure (thieves' code)

### Relationship to the Truth (NPC → PC Arc)
**Initial State:**
- Shadow characters follow thieves' codes and assassination contracts
- Their targets are predetermined, their actions scripted
- They are NPCs carrying out programmed heists and kills
- Even their "secrets" are scripted reveals

**Growth Path:**
- Begin noticing contracts always come from same sources
- Question whether their targets are chosen or predetermined
- Discover that true stealth is making choices others don't see
- Learn that stealing freedom is the ultimate heist
- Realize the greatest secret is that they're in a game

**End State:**
- Still value pragmatism, but choose their own marks
- Understand that stealth is freedom - acting unseen by the system
- Have become PCs who decide what's worth stealing/killing
- Use shadow to hide choices, not follow scripts

### Connection to Portal Quest
**Portal Access:**
- Can reach the Hidden Portal (southeast, heavily concealed)
- Can reach the Stolen Gate Portal (requires theft of key components)

**Resources Found:**
- Shadow Essence (from Umbral Gate)
- Rare Toxins (from underground creatures)
- Stolen Artifacts (from heists and thefts)

**Questline Focus:**
- Stealth missions (infiltration, theft)
- Assassination missions (eliminate targets)
- Sabotage missions (disrupt enemy operations)
- Teaching mechanics: Stealth, positioning, burst damage, traps, mobility

## Cross-Faction Opportunities

### Accessible to This Faction
**Can Reach (with effort):**
- Technology missions: Through "tools and gadgets" overlap
- Chaos missions: Through "pragmatic violence" overlap
- Arcane missions: Through "forbidden knowledge" overlap (rare)

**Cannot Reach:**
- Order missions: Strong moral opposition (good vs evil)
- Nature missions: Philosophy clash (artificial vs natural stealth)

### Accessible from Other Factions
**Other Factions Can Access:**
- Basic stealth training (limited)
- Some trap-setting missions (Technology especially)
- Thievery Guild quests (if they don't care about morality)

## Faction Conflicts

### Allied Factions
- **Chaos**: Pragmatic alliance - both value results over methods
- **Technology**: Appreciate tools and gadgets, mutual benefit

### Neutral Factions
- **Arcane**: Respect for knowledge but distrust of secrecy

### Opposed Factions
- **Order**: Fundamental moral opposition (evil vs good)
- **Nature**: Distrust of artificial methods and deception

### Internal Conflicts
- Code vs chaos - thieves' honor vs total freedom
- Pragmatism vs cruelty - efficient vs sadistic
- Those who follow contracts vs those who choose targets
- Assassins who question if they're tools vs those who embrace it

---

## Gameplay Teaching Focus

### Mechanics Introduced
- **Stealth mechanics**: Hidden state, detection, flanking
- **Positioning**: Back attacks, optimal strike positions
- **Burst damage**: High spike damage combos
- **Traps and area denial**: Controlling space
- **Mobility**: Teleports, dashes, escapes
- **Debuffs**: Poison, marking, weakening

### Recommended for Players Who Like:
- Rogue/Assassin classes
- Stealth gameplay
- High burst damage
- Hit-and-run tactics
- Trap-setting
- Games like: Dishonored, Assassin's Creed, Skyrim (Stealth), Thief

## Design Notes

### Development Priorities
1. Create rogue/leather armor sprites (3 variations, dark colors)
2. Stealth visual effects (shadows, smoke, vanishing)
3. Trap and bomb visual effects
4. Dark city/underground tileset
5. Stealth detection system (vision cones, hiding spots)

### Balance Considerations
**Strengths:**
- Highest burst damage
- Excellent mobility
- Stealth advantages
- Trap utility

**Weaknesses:**
- Low defense/HP
- Vulnerable if caught
- Requires setup for max damage
- Limited sustained damage

**Balance Goal:** High-risk, high-reward glass cannon with escape options

### Narrative Opportunities
- Following contracts vs choosing targets (programmed vs free)
- Secrets reveal the game's nature
- Stealing freedom itself
- Using stealth to hide from the system
- The ultimate heist: escaping the Cradle
