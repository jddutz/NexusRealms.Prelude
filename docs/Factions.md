# Factions

## Faction Structure

**Six Factions:**
- **At least 5 characters per faction** = 30+ total characters
- Each faction has distinct theme, abilities, and playstyles
- Each faction has at least one starting character option
- Faction relationships affect story and character availability

## Faction Design Goals

### Thematic Identity
- **Clear visual identity**: Distinct art style, color scheme, aesthetic
- **Clear mechanical identity**: Unique abilities and playstyles
- **Clear narrative identity**: Values, motivations, conflicts
- No "generic" factions - each must feel unique

### Gameplay Variety
- Unique synergies within faction
- Cross-faction combos for advanced strategies
- No single "best" faction - situational strengths
- Each faction excels in different scenarios

### Balance
- All factions viable for story completion
- Factional strengths and weaknesses
- Counter-play between factions
- Prevents one dominant strategy

## Faction Relationships

### Story Integration
- Moral decisions affect faction allegiances
- Helping one faction may lock out another
- Faction conflicts drive story choices
- Character recruitment tied to faction standing

## Faction Synergies

### Within-Faction Synergies
- Characters from same faction have natural synergies
- Thematic consistency
- Easier for new players to build effective teams
- Mono-faction parties viable

### Cross-Faction Synergies
- Advanced strategies combine multiple factions
- Rewards experimentation
- Unexpected combinations
- Higher skill ceiling
