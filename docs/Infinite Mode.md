# Infinite Mode

## Overview

**Unlocked After:**
- Defeating Final Boss
- Completing Chapter 5
- All party members have 5 skills unlocked

**Purpose:**
- Continue progression with unlocked characters
- Hundreds of hours of replayability
- Post-story endgame content
- Character optimization and build perfection

## Design Goals

### Long-Term Engagement
- **200+ hours potential** for deeply engaged players
- Maintains challenge without artificial difficulty scaling
- Rewards mastery and experimentation
- Fresh content and variety

### Progression Without RNG
- Deterministic rewards
- Skill-based advancement
- No random power spikes
- Predictable but challenging

### Build Optimization
- Test fully optimized character builds
- Experiment with synergies
- Perfect equipment loadouts
- Master advanced strategies
