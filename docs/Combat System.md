# Combat System

## Core Design Principles

### Deterministic Systems
- **Skill > Luck**: No critical hits, dodge chances, or proc rates
- Predictable outcomes allow strategic planning
- Chess-like predictability where player mastery drives success
- No artificial difficulty through random chance

### Battle Length
- **Target: 10 minutes per battle**
- Long enough for tactical depth
- Short enough for multiple battles per session
- 3 battles per run = ~30 minute run sessions

## Enemy Design

### AI & Tactics
- **Smart AI** (not just bigger numbers)
- Enemies use actual tactics
- Enemy synergies (like player party synergies)
- Predictable but challenging behavior
- Different enemy factions with distinct playstyles

### Enemy Composition
- Varied enemy types per battle
- Counter-synergies to player strategies
- Forces adaptation and learning
- No single dominant strategy

## Difficulty System

### Challenge Without Scaling
- **No artificial HP/damage scaling**
- **No random ability removal**
- Difficulty through:
  - Smarter enemy AI
  - Complex enemy compositions
  - Tactical scenarios/puzzles
  - Optional objectives
  - Resource constraints

### Skill-Based Difficulty
- Enemy patterns to learn
- Positioning challenges
- Timing/sequencing puzzles
- Synergy requirements
- Multiple valid solutions

## Battle Outcomes

### Victory
- Progress story
- Gain rewards (items, currency)
- Skill improvement opportunities
- Move to post-battle flow

### Defeat/Retreat
- Can restart that battle
- Can give up (losses are permanent)
- Main character death: Must restart that battle
- No save scumming

### NPC Support
- Some battles include NPC allies
- **[TO BE DETERMINED]**: How does this work mechanically?
- Do NPCs have full abilities?
- Can player control them?

## Synergy & Team Composition

### Party Synergies
- Abilities combo with other party members
- Equipment enhances synergies
- Faction bonuses
- Position-based bonuses
- Timing-based combos

### Build Crafting
- Different party compositions enable different strategies
- High risk / high reward strategies viable
- Experimentation encouraged
- No "mandatory" party composition
