The twelve Zodiac specializations

Aries — The Conqueror
Fire · Cardinal · Mars
Aggression, physical force, momentum, and battlefield displacement. Aries overwhelms opponents through direct attacks, knockback, and disruption of enemy formations.
Signature: Forceful strikes, Crushing damage, Move, Delay, multi-target melee.

Taurus — The Bulwark
Earth · Fixed · Venus
Stability, persistence, endurance, and accumulated strength. Taurus survives punishment, holds its ground, and becomes increasingly difficult to overcome.
Signature: Resist, Negate, Crushing damage, defensive statuses, counterattacks.

Gemini — The Arcanist
Air · Mutable · Mercury
Duality, intelligence, adaptability, and interconnected effects. Gemini manipulates magical energy, combining spells and propagating effects between enemies.
Signature: Electrical damage, Chain Lightning, Focus manipulation, Modify, multi-target propagation.

Cancer — The Guardian
Water · Cardinal · Moon
Protection, restoration, resilience, and emotional strength. Cancer specializes in surviving dangerous encounters through defensive and restorative techniques.
Signature: Restore, Resist, Negate, reactive protection, restorative statuses.

Leo — The Sovereign
Fire · Fixed · Sun
Confidence, authority, radiance, and dominance. Leo projects power through Fire, imposing its presence and punishing enemies that challenge it.
Signature: Fire damage, area attacks, intimidation, retaliatory effects, empowering statuses.

Virgo — The Alchemist
Earth · Mutable · Mercury
Precision, analysis, refinement, and practical knowledge. Virgo identifies vulnerabilities and manipulates physical conditions, using carefully prepared effects.
Signature: Piercing damage, targeted Modify, debuffs, Dispel, persistent weakening statuses.

Libra — The Arbiter
Air · Cardinal · Venus
Balance, fairness, symmetry, and consequence. Libra manipulates the relationship between offense and defense, redistributing advantages and disadvantages.
Signature: Transfer, Modify, Negate, reciprocal effects, damage redistribution.

Scorpio — The Reaper
Water · Fixed · Mars / Pluto
Secrecy, intensity, transformation, and hidden power. Scorpio weakens enemies through persistent afflictions, then exploits their vulnerability.
Signature: Void damage, Piercing, Consume, persistent statuses, conditional finishing attacks.

Sagittarius — The Marksman
Fire · Mutable · Jupiter
Exploration, freedom, distance, and ambition. Sagittarius excels at ranged combat, exploiting weaknesses and striking targets beyond ordinary reach.
Signature: Ranged attacks, Piercing, Fire, target selection, Focus-powered precision.

Capricorn — The Strategist
Earth · Cardinal · Saturn
Discipline, patience, structure, and long-term planning. Capricorn controls the tempo of combat, accumulating advantages through carefully timed actions.
Signature: Delay, Restrict, Turn manipulation, defensive preparation, conditional damage.

Aquarius — The Disruptor
Air · Fixed · Saturn / Uranus
Innovation, rebellion, unconventional thinking, and transformation. Aquarius changes the rules of engagement through unusual status interactions and disruptions to enemy actions.
Signature: Electrical and Aether damage, Restrict, Dispel, Modify, unconventional targeting.

Pisces — The Mystic
Water · Mutable · Jupiter / Neptune
Intuition, dreams, fluidity, and transcendence. Pisces specializes in flowing elemental effects, Ice, Spirit, and evasion through magical means.
Signature: Ice and Spirit damage, Evade, slowing statuses, flowing area effects, Restore.

## What the elements and modalities represent

|          | Traditional associations                        |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Fire     | Energy, passion, action, inspiration            |
| Earth    | Stability, practicality, material reality       |
| Air      | Intellect, communication, ideas, relationships  |
| Water    | Emotion, intuition, sensitivity, transformation |
| Cardinal | Initiation, leadership, creating change         |
| Fixed    | Persistence, stability, maintaining direction   |
| Mutable  | Adaptability, flexibility, transformation       |

This gives us a useful foundation for combat specialization.

For example, Aries, Leo, and Sagittarius share Fire's aggressive and energetic qualities, but their modalities suggest different expressions:

- Aries (Cardinal): Initiates combat, creates openings, forces movement.
- Leo (Fixed): Maintains dominance, establishes persistent empowering effects.
- Sagittarius (Mutable): Adapts offensively, exploits range and changing opportunities.

The same distinction applies to the other three elemental groups.

This elemental and modal structure may be more useful than assigning traditional RPG classes, because it gives us twelve distinct combinations of combat philosophy without prescribing specific weapons, spells, or damage types.
